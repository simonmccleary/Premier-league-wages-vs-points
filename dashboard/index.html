How it was built, and what to be careful about

  • Results come from football-data.co.uk. Staff costs come from the notes to each club's accounts at Companies House, read from the page images and checked so that wages, social security and pension costs add to the printed total.
  • Staff costs are the club's total employee costs, not player wages alone. They include commercial and administrative staff, and some clubs report group rather than company figures. Treat small gaps between clubs as noise.
  • Periods of 11, 13 or 14 months (year-end changes, the 2020 extension) are scaled to 12 months. The toggle above shows the figures as reported.
  • The model is points = a + b * ln(staff costs / season average), with errors clustered by club. It describes association. It does not show that spending causes points.
  • Averages for clubs with only three or four seasons move a lot. Brentford's gap, for example, rests on four seasons.
